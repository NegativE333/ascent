## Intro card (Explore only, not a clue)
- Dam = structure across a river to store water, control floods,
  generate power, irrigate — often multipurpose. Nehru called dams
  "Temples of Modern India." Drawbacks: displacement, biodiversity loss,
  inter-state/international water disputes.
- Hydroelectric generation: stored water's potential energy → kinetic
  energy through the penstock → turbine → generator → electricity.
- Waterfall types: Cataract (large volume, powerful), Plunge (falls
  clear of the cliff face), Cascade (stepped/multi-drop).

## Ramsar sites — summary only, not individual clues
93 sites total (Oct 2025). First two: Chilika Lake (Odisha) and Keoladeo
National Park (Rajasthan). Most sites: Tamil Nadu. Latest additions: Tawa
Reservoir (MP), Nanjarayan & Kazhuveli (TN). Montreux Record = wetlands
whose ecological character is at risk from pollution/human interference.
